## 4. Strongest statement actually proved

Let Delta be a finite-dimensional Q-division algebra with Delta_R = prod K_v, O an order with every nonzero LEFT ideal principal, (G_m) a coherent family ((G1) normal in GL_m(Delta_R) and ||.|| = 1; (G2) stabiliser of e_1 is {u(n,h): h in G_{m-1}}), Gamma_m = G_m cap GL_m(O), 1 <= j < k. ASSUME, for each m in {k-j+1, ..., k} (all >= 2): Gamma_m is a lattice in G_m and the Siegel identity holds for f in C_c with the invariant probability measure on Gamma_m\G_m. THEN for every Borel F: Delta_R^{j x k} -> [0, infinity], the mean over Gamma_k\G_k of the sum of F(Y gamma) over Y in O^{j x k} of Delta-rank j equals the integral of F (Lebesgue measure with covol(O^{j x k}) = 1), as an identity in [0, infinity]. Coherent families include prod_v SL_m(K_v), {det_R = 1}, {|det_R| = 1}; for Delta_R = H x H the group {N = 1} of R14 is one of them.
