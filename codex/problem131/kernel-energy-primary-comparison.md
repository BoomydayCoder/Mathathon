# Primary-source comparison for the logarithmic-kernel route

This records a bounded primary-source comparison, not a novelty search with
an exclusion guarantee. The kernel, rational square, Legendre convolution,
orthogonal forms, and Apéry recurrence are classical. The present notes
independently reconstruct their connections and simplify one route through
an explicit positive differential energy.

## Borwein, Erdélyi and Zhang

The author-hosted PDF *Orthogonality and Irrationality*, printed version dated
3 November 2013, is archived as `sources/borwein-erdelyi-zhang-orthogonality.pdf`.
Source: https://people.tamu.edu/~terdelyi/papers-online/zeta.pdf .
The exact content hash and retrieval information are in
`sources/kernel-energy-primary-search.json`.

Theorem 1 constructs repeated-exponent Müntz orthogonal forms by a contour
integral, with polynomial times logarithm components and explicit coefficient
integrality. Section 3, Theorem 4 and Lemmas 1–3 identify the zeta3 rational
form and the positive Beukers integral, obtaining its classical geometric
bound. The introduction explicitly unifies log2, zeta2, zeta3 and polylogarithm
irrationality through this orthogonality. Thus neither this unification nor
the logarithmic-form/Beukers link is a new construction of this session.
Their displayed proof gets the estimate from Rodrigues/Padé transformations
and the cube maximum. Our short note gets a sufficient estimate from the
kernel differential equation and positive energy. This is a comparison of
these written arguments, not a claim that the latter route is absent elsewhere.

## Arvesú

The institutional PDF *Orthogonal forms: a simple tool for proving the
irrationality of zeta(3)*, dated24 August2009, is archived as
`sources/arvesu-orthogonal-forms-2009.pdf`.
Source: https://e-archivo.uc3m.es/bitstreams/e46c370d-635b-4313-9075-80a0c10699f8/download .
This is the retrieved draft version; conclusions about its wording do not
claim to describe every later publication or revision.

Section4.1, equations(54)–(57), explicitly identifies the Legendre Mellin
convolution with a polynomial plus a polynomial times log(x). It then
converts the logarithmic double integral to the Beukers triple integral.
Section3.1 uses orthogonal forms to prove nonvanishing and a Casoratian
identity, then discusses the Apéry recurrence. The recurrence coefficient
calculation on printed pp18–19 introduces a cubic ansatz and fits four
values. Those four values alone do not justify the all-index cubic ansatz;
we do not use that passage as a proof of our recurrence. Our independent
commuting-grid derivation proves the coefficients for every index and keeps
the source at zero explicit. This observation is limited to that specific
passage and is not a claim that Apéry's recurrence is in doubt.

## Present claim boundary

The accepted kernel notes prove: the unit boundary flux, polynomial division
forcing the squared binomial sum, positive-energy smallness, reciprocal
Legendre conjugacy, and exact compatibility of the two index grids. They
identify their underlying classical objects and make no historical novelty
claim. The existing Prévost1996 and Ismail–Koelink dictionaries remain the
primary matches for the Wilson/Padé and differential-operator families.
Further exact historical identification of the energy route is unresolved.
