            SIMULTANEOUS GENERATION OF KOECHER AND
            ALMKVIST-GRANVILLE’S APÉRY-LIKE FORMULAE

                                                     T. RIVOAL

       Abstract. We prove a very general identity, conjectured by Henri Cohen, involving the
       generating function of the familly (ζ(2r + 4s + 3))r,s≥0 : it unifies two identities, proved
       by Koecher in 1980 and Almkvist & Granville in 1999, for the generating functions of
       (ζ(2r + 3))r≥0 and (ζ(4s + 3))s≥0 respectively. As a consequence, we obtain that, for any
       integer j ≥ 0, there exist at least [j/2] + 1 Apéry-like formulae for ζ(2j + 3).
