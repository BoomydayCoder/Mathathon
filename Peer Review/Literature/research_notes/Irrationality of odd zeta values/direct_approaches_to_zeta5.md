## Key question 1 — Multiple integrals (Vasilenko/Vasilyev, Zudilin, Zlobin, Sorokin, Fischler, Cresson–Fischler–Rivoal)

### Takeaway
The Beukers-type 5-fold integrals of Vasilyev (and their Sorokin/Zlobin/Fischler/Zudilin reformulations as very-well-poised hypergeometric series) give explicit small linear forms in 1, ζ(3), ζ(5) with denominator d_n^5; they prove only that 1, ζ(3), ζ(5) span a space of dimension ≥ 2, because there is no way to eliminate ζ(3) without destroying the "decay beats denominators" balance. Generic 5-fold integrals of this kind are worse: they produce weight-5 multiple zeta values (ζ(3,2), ζ(2)ζ(3)) and even zeta values as parasitic terms.

**1.3 Krattenthaler–Rivoal — denominators and a second proof of Zudilin's identity.**

Proves Rivoal's "denominator conjecture": for S_{n,A,B,C,r}(z) = Σ_k (1/C!) ∂^C/∂k^C [n!^{A−2Br}(k+n/2)(k−rn)_{rn}^B (k+n+1)_{rn}^B/(k)_{n+1}^A] z^{−k}, one has d_n^{A+C} p_{0,C,n} ∈ Z[X] and d_n^{A−l} p_{l,n} ∈ Z[X] (instead of the trivially available higher powers); Vasilyev's conjecture (ii) is a corollary; also proves Zudilin's conjecture on ζ(4) forms. ### Inferences
- Decay versus denominators for the Vasilyev/Zudilin forms in 1, ζ(3), ζ(5): by Zudilin's 2002 note (next section) the very-well-poised form r_n = u_nζ(5) + w_nζ(3) − v_n satisfies lim log|r_n|/n = log λ_1 where λ_1 is the smallest root of λ³ − 188λ² − 2368λ + 4, i.e. λ_1 ≈ 0.0016890, log λ_1 ≈ −6.3836, and 2u_n, 2D_n²w_n, 2D_n⁵v_n ∈ Z. Hence D_n⁵ r_n ≈ e^{(5 − 6.38)n} → 0: a genuinely small integer linear form in 1, ζ(3), ζ(5) (my arithmetic from the published characteristic polynomial; not a quotation). This is exactly why the construction proves "dimension ≥ 2" but cannot isolate ζ(5): there is one small form per n and two unknown irrationals.
- The multiple-integral line (Vasilyev/Zlobin/Sorokin/Fischler) and the hypergeometric line (Zudilin, Krattenthaler–Rivoal) are reformulations of the same linear forms (Zudilin's identity), not independent constructions; what each adds is a different handle on denominators (integral side: d_n^E; series side: d_n^{E+1} until Krattenthaler–Rivoal).
- Generic 5-fold integrals are strictly worse than the well-poised/Vasilyev ones because of weight-5 MZVs and even zetas; the only known mechanisms that remove unwanted terms are (a) well-poised symmetry, (b) the geometric (anti-)invariance of Dupont, (c) Brown's cohomological vanishing — none removes ζ(3) from a small form in 1, ζ(3), ζ(5).

## Key question 2 — Recurrences and continued fractions for ζ(5)

### Takeaway
The only Apéry-like recurrence known to be attached to ζ(5) before 2026 is Zudilin's third-order recursion (2002), obtained by eliminating ζ(3) between two well-poised forms; it yields p_n/q_n → ζ(5) with |q_nζ(5) − p_n| ≈ e^{−1.086n} but denominators 2D_n^5 ≈ e^{5n}, so the cleared forms grow like e^{3.9n}; Brown–Zudilin later showed the same sequences are the totally symmetric cellular integrals on M_{0,8} (worthiness 0.778 after an extra binomial normalisation). No second-order (Apéry-type) recurrence for ζ(5) alone is known in the retrieved literature.

### Cited Findings

**2.1 Zudilin, "A third-order Apéry-like recursion for ζ(5)".**
- W. Zudilin, arXiv:math/0206178 (27 May 2002, rev. 6 July 2002), 5 pp.; Mat. Zametki 72:5 (2002), 796–800; Math. Notes 72:5 (2002), 733–737; DOI 10.1023/A:1021473409544. Peer-reviewed short note (retrieved in full). — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0206178), [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0206178)
- Recursion (1): (n+1)^6 a_0(n) q_{n+1} + a_1(n) q_n − 4(2n−1) a_2(n) q_{n−1} − 4(n−1)^4(2n−1)(2n−3) a_0(n+1) q_{n−2} = 0, with a_0(n) = 41218n³ − 48459n² + 20010n − 2871 and explicit a_1 (degree 9), a_2 (degree 8); three solutions q_n, p_n, p̃_n with q_0 = −1, q_1 = 42, q_2 = −17934; p_1 = 87/2, p_2 = −1190161/64; p̃_1 = 101/2, p̃_2 = −344923/16. — [arXiv:math/0206178, §1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0206178)
- Theorem 1: ℓ_n = q_nζ(5) − p_n > 0 and ℓ̃_n = q_nζ(3) − p̃_n < 0 satisfy lim log|ℓ_n|/n = lim log|ℓ̃_n|/n = log|μ_2| = −1.08607936…, while lim log|q_n|/n = log|μ_3|, where μ_1 = −0.02001512…, μ_2 = 0.33753726…, μ_3 = −2368.31752213… are the roots of μ³ + 2368μ² − 752μ − 16. Convergence p_n/q_n → ζ(5) at rate |μ_2/μ_3| < 1.42521964·10^{−4} (e.g. p_1/q_1 = 29/28, p_2/q_2 = 24289/23424; error < 5.52·10^{−192} at n = 50). — [arXiv:math/0206178, Theorem 1 and table](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0206178)
- Denominators: the construction gives 4D_n²q_n, 4D_n⁷p_n, 4D_n⁵p̃_n ∈ Z, while "straightforward calculations on the basis of the recursion" show q_n ∈ Z, 2D_n⁵p_n ∈ Z, 2D_n³p̃_n ∈ Z. Author's verdict: "The inclusions (6) do not allow oneself to prove the irrationality of the number ζ(5), hence we do not accent our attention on arithmetic properties … and stress only on an applied meaning of Theorem 1" (a fast algorithm for computing ζ(5)). — [arXiv:math/0206178, eq. (6)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0206178)
- Method: two very-well-poised series r_n = n!^4 Σ_k (k + n/2) ∏_{j=1}^n (k−j) ∏_{j=1}^n (k+n+j) / ∏_{j=0}^n (k+j)^6 and r̃_n (with ∏_{j=0}^n) are Q-linear forms u_nζ(5) + w_nζ(3) − v_n, ũ_nζ(5) + w̃_nζ(3) − ṽ_n (r_0 = ζ(5), r_1 = 9ζ(5) + 33ζ(3) − 49, r̃_0 = ζ(3), r̃_1 = 2ζ(5) + 12ζ(3) − 33/2); creative telescoping (Zeilberger) gives third-order recursions for each, with common characteristic polynomial λ³ − 188λ² − 2368λ + 4 (roots λ_1, λ_2, λ_3; μ_1 = λ_1λ_2, μ_2 = λ_1λ_3, μ_3 = λ_2λ_3); log|r_n|/n → log λ_1, coefficients grow like λ_3^n; by Vasilyev's result and Zudilin's identity, 2u_n ∈ Z, 2D_n²w_n ∈ Z, 2D_n⁵v_n ∈ Z. ζ(3) is then eliminated by the 2×2 determinants q_n = u_nw̃_n − ũ_nw_n, p_n = w̃_nv_n − w_nṽ_n, p̃_n = u_nṽ_n − ũ_nv_n; asymptotics by Poincaré's theorem. — [arXiv:math/0206178, §2, Theorems 2–3, eqs. (7)–(15)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0206178)
- Companion statement in the same note: an analogous third-order recursion (16) for simultaneous approximations to ζ(2) and ζ(3) (characteristic polynomial μ³ − 220μ² + 32μ − 16; q'_n ∈ Z, D_n³p'_n ∈ Z, D_n²p̃'_n ∈ Z). **2.2 Re-identification by Brown–Zudilin (2022/2026).**

### Inferences
- Exact margin for Zudilin's 2002 sequence (my arithmetic from the quoted constants): after clearing 2D_n⁵, D_n⁵|q_nζ(5) − p_n| ≈ e^{(5 − 1.0861)n} = e^{3.914n} → ∞; an irrationality proof would need decay faster than e^{−5n}. In worthiness terms c_0 = 3.914, c_1 = log 2368.3175 + 5 = 12.770, γ ≈ 0.6935; Brown–Zudilin's removal of the common factor binom(2n,n) (growth 4^n) improves this to the 0.778 they state, and the full M_{0,8} parameter family to 0.866.
- The root structure explains the loss: eliminating ζ(3) between two forms each of size λ_1^n with coefficients of size λ_3^n produces a form of size (λ_1λ_3)^n = 0.3375^n — the "subleading" asymptotics — while the denominators stay at D_n⁵. In Apéry's ζ(3) case the form itself is the minimal (Perron) solution of a second-order recursion; here the ζ(5)-form sits at the middle root of a third-order recursion.
- The reciprocal relation between Zudilin's λ³ − 188λ² − 2368λ + 4 and Brown–Zudilin's 4λ³ − 2368λ² − 188λ + 1 (λ ↦ 1/λ) reflects Brown's duality 8π8 ↔ 8π8∨ between the Vasilyev forms in 1, ζ(3), ζ(5) and the cellular forms in 1, ζ(2), ζ(5) + 2ζ(2)ζ(3) (inference from the two retrieved papers; Brown states the duality, not the polynomial relation).

## Key question 3 — Moduli-space and motivic approaches (Brown, Brown–Zudilin, Dupont, follow-ups)

### Takeaway
Brown's cellular integrals on M_{0,n} give a common geometric framework (Apéry for n = 5, 6; Ball–Rivoal; Vasilyev's forms as the configuration 8π8) and a mechanism ("set ζ(2) to zero" on the dual family 8π8∨) producing honest linear forms in 1 and ζ(5); Brown–Zudilin (2022, revised Jan 2026) pushed this, with a 7!-element symmetry group controlling denominators, to the best pre-2026 result: effective approximations with |ζ(5) − p/q| < q^{−0.86}, explicitly not an irrationality proof (the cleared forms grow like e^{18.05n} for the optimal parameters).

### Cited Findings

**3.1 Brown, "Irrationality proofs for zeta values, moduli spaces and dinner parties".**
- F. Brown, arXiv:1412.6508 (19 Dec 2014); Mosc. J. Comb. Number Theory 6 (2016), no. 2–3, 102–165 (journal data from Brown–Zudilin ref. [6] and Lai ref. [7]). Peer-reviewed; retrieved in full. — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.6508), [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.6508)
- Result: period integrals on M_{0,n} attached to "convergent configurations" (a variant of the dinner-table problem) reproduce Apéry's approximations to ζ(2), ζ(3) and, for larger n, give infinite families of small linear forms in multiple zeta values, including a generalisation of the Ball–Rivoal forms. Four requirements for a proof are isolated: small forms, controlled denominators, linear independence criteria, and "(4) Vanishing theorems for some of the coefficients". — [arXiv:1412.6508, abstract and §1.3–1.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.6508)
- Size of the search space for ζ(5): "finding good linear forms in zeta values amongst the integrals (1.2) is significantly harder than finding a needle in a haystack. For example, the general integral yielding linear forms in multiple zeta values of weight at most 5 (of interest, if one seeks linear forms in 1 and ζ(5)) depends on 20 independent parameters, which is hopelessly large." — [arXiv:1412.6508, §1.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.6508)
- Parasitic terms: "the generic integral (1.1) contains all multiple zeta values of weight up to and including ℓ. Thus, the presence of terms such as ζ(2n) … blocks any further progress." — [arXiv:1412.6508, §1.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.6508)
- N = 8 census (experimental): 17 convergent configurations; table of which of 1, ζ(2), ζ(3), ζ(4), ζ(5), ζ(3)ζ(2) occur. The ζ(4) coefficient always vanishes; "There is a unique entry, 8π8 = 8π_odd which gives a linear form in 1, ζ(3), ζ(5) only … The dual 8π8∨ of this sequence gives linear forms in 1, ζ(2) and 2ζ(5) + 4ζ(3)ζ(2) from which we can extract linear forms in 1, ζ(5)". For N = 9 (105 configuration classes) all forms vanish in weight 5; N = 10 has 771 configurations with several vanishing patterns. — [arXiv:1412.6508, §10.2.4–10.2.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.6508)
- §7.1, Example 7.5 (the seed of Brown–Zudilin): for 8π8∨ a generalised cellular integral equals a_0 + a_1ζ(2) + a_2ζ_5 with ζ_5 = 2ζ(2)ζ(3) + ζ(5); projecting motivic ζ(2) to zero gives a_0 + a_2ζ(5) with a_2 = 2407028604043866880, a_0 = −48144548550856003417243773593/19289340000; "The Z-linear form obtained by clearing denominators is less than 1, which is what is required for an irrationality proof. There are many similar examples. An infinite family of such examples would suffice to prove the irrationality of ζ(5)." — [arXiv:1412.6508, Example 7.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.6508)
- §5 examples for the generalised 8π-family I(a_1,…,a_7,b): experimentally linear in 1, ζ(3), ζ(5) only; sporadic cases with vanishing ζ(3) coefficient, e.g. I(1,0,0,1,0,0,0,0) = 2ζ(5) − 2, I(2,0,0,2,0,0,0,0) = 2ζ(5) − 33/16, I(3,2,0,3,2,0,2,2) = 60ζ(5) − 161263/2592, which "could be part of an infinite sequence of approximations to ζ(5) … or could just be accidental". — [arXiv:1412.6508, §5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.6508)
- Author's own assessment: "The methods described here also lead to new approximations to single odd zeta values such as ζ(5) (§7.1) but it is unclear if they could lead to an irrationality proof." Also: "one might hope to prove the transcendence of ζ(3) by optimizing our polynomial forms in ζ(3) along the lines of [Sorokin]". — [arXiv:1412.6508, §1.7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.6508)
- Prerequisites/useful sections: §1 (programme), §3 (dinner-table configurations), §7 (Ball–Fischler–Rivoal integrals and dual forms), §8 (cohomological vanishing), §10 (tables for N ≤ 10), §11 (vanishing lemmas).

**3.2 Brown–Zudilin, "On cellular rational approximations to ζ(5)".**
- F. Brown, W. Zudilin, arXiv:2210.03391 (v1 7 Oct 2022; v3 29 Jan 2026, "Revised: 26 January 2026", 32 pp.). Status: arXiv preprint; v3 acknowledges "creative feedback from the anonymous referee" but the arXiv record carries no journal reference as of 15 Nov 2026. Retrieved in full (v3). — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03391), [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.03391)
- Theorem 1 (exact statement): "There is an effective infinite sequence of rational approximations p/q, with p, q ∈ Z, to ζ(5) such that 0 < |ζ(5) − p/q| < 1/q^{0.86}." "Effective" (after Nesterenko) means p_n, q_n satisfy a linear recursion with polynomial coefficients; trivial approximations only give exponent < 1 ineffectively. "Note that this result does not imply the (expected!) irrationality of ζ(5) which would follow if the upper bound … were of the form 1/q^{1+ε}… The 'worthiness' exponent 0.86 is nevertheless best possible when compared to any other known constructions of effective rational approximations to ζ(5)". — [arXiv:2210.03391v3, Theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.03391)
- Construction: the 8-parameter family I(a_1,…,a_8) of 5-fold cellular integrals of type 8π8∨ on M_{0,8} (convergent iff seventeen linear forms in the a_i are ≥ 0). A priori a combination of 1, ζ(2), ζ(3), ζ(4), ζ(5), ζ(3)ζ(2); by Poincaré duality the ζ(4) term vanishes, ζ(5) and ζ(2)ζ(3) occur only as ζ(5) + 2ζ(3)ζ(2), and the ζ(3) coefficient vanishes, so I = Q·(2ζ(5) + 4ζ(3)ζ(2)) − 4P̂·ζ(2) − 2P with Q ∈ Z. The integral I itself only shows that one of ζ(2), ζ(5) + 2ζ(3)ζ(2) is irrational ("the number ζ(2) is usually viewed as 'parasitic'"). Key idea: I = 2I' + 4I''ζ(2) with I' = Qζ(5) − P and I'' = Qζ(3) − P̂, i.e. the cellular integral is "in disguise, a pair of simultaneous approximations I', I'' to ζ(5) and ζ(3)"; "setting ζ(2) to zero" is justified motivically/cohomologically. — [arXiv:2210.03391v3, §1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.03391)
- Group structure: a transformation group G of order 7! = 5040 acts on normalised integrals and on Q, P, P̂ (a "group structure for ζ(5)" in the Rhin–Viola sense), giving a sharp denominator D(a) with D·I' ∈ Zζ(5) + Z; G is shown to be exhaustive. — [arXiv:2210.03391v3, §1, §8, §12](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.03391)
- Proof of Theorem 1, numbers for a = (8,16,10,15,12,16,18,13)·n: gain from the factorial prefactors lim log Φ_n/n = 34.39425186…; lim log|I(an)|/n = −66.05784567…; lim log|Q_nζ(5) − P_n|/n = −31.55296934…; lim log Q_n/n = 85.08768883…; lcm-type denominators contributing m_1 + … + m_5 = 18 + 17 + 17 + 16 + 16 = 84 to the exponential rate (m_1 = 18, m_2 = m_3 = 17, m_4 = m_5 = 16). "Because −31.55296934… + 18 + 17 + 17 + 16 + 16 − 34.39425186… > 0, we cannot make any conclusions about the irrationality of ζ(5)." With C_0 = −31.553, C_1 = 85.088, C_2 = 84 − 34.394, γ(a) = (C_1 − C_0)/(C_1 + C_2) = 0.86597135…. — [arXiv:2210.03391v3, §11](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.03391)
- Documented limitations and open ends (§12 "(In)conclusive comments"): the p-adic exponents from G are not always optimal (e.g. for a = (15,20,16,14,18,17,16,20), γ = 0.85163139… provable, 0.85665016… experimentally); extra savings are expected "from different integral representations, each possessing their own arithmetic" as in Zudilin's ζ(2)/ζ(3) work, "But what are those other representations?"; "The price to pay was that one must pass to the subleading asymptotic"; higher-weight integrals are not computable in practice with current tools. A 7-fold analogue on M_{0,10} (configuration (10,2,4,1,6,3,8,5,9,7)) gives I_n = I'_n + I''_nζ(2) with I'_n linear in 1, ζ(5), ζ(7) and I''_n linear in 1, ζ(3), ζ(5); "a similar analysis … might possibly lead to a result of the form 'at least one of ζ(5) and ζ(7) is irrational'". Closing sentence: "the interplay between these different mathematical structures … lies at the heart of the difficulty of finding new irrationality proofs." — [arXiv:2210.03391v3, §12](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.03391)
- Opening assessment (still in the January 2026 revision): "the traditional strategy for approaching the irrationality of ζ(5) is to try to construct linear forms in 1 and ζ(5) with rational coefficients and good arithmetic properties. At the time of writing, it has not succeeded despite many years of effort." — [arXiv:2210.03391v3, §1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.03391)
- Connections: totally symmetric case = Zudilin's 2002 recursion (see 2.2); tools: Panzer's HyperInt, Koutschan's HolonomicFunctions, Rhin–Viola groups, Zudilin's "Two hypergeometric tales" (below).

**3.3 Dupont, "Odd zeta motive and linear forms in odd zeta values".**
- C. Dupont, arXiv:1601.00950 (v3 June 2018); Compositio Math. 154 (2018), 342–379; DOI 10.1112/S0010437X17007588; with a joint appendix with D. Zagier. Peer-reviewed; retrieved. — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.00950), [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1601.00950)
- Result: a family of mixed Tate motives over Z whose periods are linear forms in ζ(n), including the Beukers–Rhin–Viola integrals for ζ(2) and the Ball–Rivoal forms; a general integral formula for the coefficients; "a geometric interpretation of the vanishing of the coefficients of a given parity" via an involution τ: if τ.ω = ω then a_k(ω) = 0 for k ≠ 0 even, if τ.ω = −ω then a_k(ω) = 0 for k odd; applies to ∫_{[0,1]^n} ∏ x_i^{u_i−1}(1−x_i)^{v_i−1}/(1 − x_1⋯x_n)^N with 2u_i + v_i = N + 1. ### Inferences
- "Margin" in Brown–Zudilin for the optimal parameters (my arithmetic from their constants): the integer forms D·(Q_nζ(5) − P_n) have exponential rate −31.553 + 84 − 34.394 = +18.053 per n, against a denominator rate of 49.606; irrationality would need this to be negative, i.e. an extra saving of more than 18.05 (about 36% of the remaining denominator) or correspondingly faster decay. The full integral I(an) (rate −66.058) would beat the denominators by 16.45, but it contains ζ(2); the loss from −66.06 to −31.55 is the price of "passing to the subleading asymptotic".
- Caveat on Brown–Zudilin eq. (6) (my own check, not a statement in the paper): with the paper's listed values P_1 = 87/4, P_2 = 1190161/384, P̂_1 = 101/4 one gets d_1⁵P_1 = 87/4, d_2⁵P_2 = 1190161/12 and d_1²d_2P̂_1 = 101/2, so (6) as printed in v3 does not hold literally at n = 1, 2, whereas Zudilin's 2002 inclusion 2D_n⁵p_n ∈ Z (with p_n = ±binom(2n,n)P_n) does. Either a normalising factor is implicit (the paper's footnote 1 allows rescaling p_n, q_n by a common rational factor) or there is a misprint. If a factor of size binom(2n,n) ≈ 4^n were really needed, the symmetric-case worthiness would drop from the stated 0.778 to about 0.69; the symmetric-case figure should therefore be quoted as "stated by the authors on the basis of an experimental observation". Theorem 1 (0.86) rests on the separately derived denominators D(a) from the group G, not on eq. (6).
- The three geometric items are genuinely different in kind: Brown (framework + vanishing by cohomology of M_{0,n}), Dupont (parity vanishing through an involution on specific motives), Brown–Zudilin (quantitative exploitation of one family). Brown–Zudilin's symmetric case is a reformulation of Zudilin 2002, and 8π8 is a reformulation of Vasilyev's forms.
- Brown's 2026 Mellin/transfinite-diameter paper and Zudilin's 2017 determinant criterion are the conceptual antecedents that the 2026 Hankel-determinant papers themselves name (see Key question 6); neither contains a ζ(5) result.

## Key question 4 — Experimental and computer-assisted searches

### Takeaway
None of the experimental programmes produced a proof or even a candidate proof for ζ(5): Dougherty-Bliss–Koutschan–Zeilberger found candidates only for other constants; Zeilberger–Zudilin and Chamberland–Straub do not treat ζ(5) beyond naming it as the open target; the Ramanujan Machine group found polynomial continued fractions for combinations of ζ(5) and ζ(3) and reported that they had not found a conservative matrix field for ζ(5), with a best "δ ≈ −0.717" (needs δ > 0).

## Key question 5 — Structural explanations of the difficulty

### Takeaway
The retrieved literature gives a consistent diagnosis: (i) natural weight-5 periods are linear forms in several numbers (ζ(3), ζ(2), ζ(2)ζ(3), ζ(3,2)) and removing the parasitic ones costs a move to subleading asymptotics; (ii) denominators grow like d_n^5 = e^{5n} (weight = number of d_n's) while available decay rates do not keep up, and constructions get worse as weight grows; (iii) the classical criterion needs ε·Δ < 1, and Zudilin (2017) showed that Hankel determinants of a structured sequence relax this to ε·Δ^{3/2}/4 < 1 with a transfinite-diameter obstruction to going further in one variable. Expert MathOverflow answers could not be retrieved.

### Inferences
- A compact way to state the pre-2026 obstruction: for a weight-5 period the natural denominator is d_n^5 (rate 5 per unit of n in the totally symmetric normalisation), so a linear form in 1 and ζ(5) with integer leading coefficient must decay faster than e^{−5n}; the forms actually available after eliminating ζ(3) or ζ(2) decay like e^{−1.09n} (Zudilin 2002) or e^{−2.47n} (Brown–Zudilin symmetric), versus Apéry's 3.5255 > 3 in weight 3.
- Zudilin's 2017 criterion already shows the logical shape of the eventual 2026 argument: replace linear forms by Hankel determinants (polynomials in ξ of growing degree), prove non-vanishing by positivity (Heine/Andréief), and gain in the denominator bookkeeping; what it lacked for ζ(5) was a sequence satisfying its hypotheses with εΔ^{3/2}/4 < 1 — the paper offers no such sequence for ζ(5) or Catalan. Brown (2026) explicitly disputes Zudilin's pessimism about higher-dimensional gains ("we find that his remark is unduly pessimistic"). The Fauzan construction (Key question 6) departs from Zudilin's hypotheses by using a different entry structure (moments of a positive weight against rational functions with poles at −j²) and by proving determinant-level p-adic estimates much stronger than entrywise bounds — as summarised by Lai. This comparison is my synthesis of the retrieved texts.
- Calegari–Dimitrov–Tang's comment implies that "worthiness" is not the right invariant for holonomy-bound methods: a construction with γ < 1 could in principle still be usable if its generating functions have favourable analytic continuation ("overconvergence"); they did not carry this out for ζ(5).

**6.3 arXiv:2611.16156 — verified.**
- S. Fischler, L. Lai, J. Sprang, W. Zudilin, "Many more odd zeta values are irrational", arXiv:2611.16156v1 [math.NT], submitted 12 Nov 2026, 17 pp. Preprint (three days old at search date). Theorem 1.1: for ε > 0 and large odd s, the number N_s of irrational values among ζ(3),…,ζ(s) satisfies N_s ≥ (√(4ζ(2)ζ(3)/ζ(6)) − ε)·√(s log log s / log s), with √(4ζ(2)ζ(3)/ζ(6)) = 2.788258…, improving the Lai–Yu bound 1.19√(s/log s) (constant later 1.28) by a factor √(log log s). — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16156), [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.16156)
- What it says about ζ(5) and the method: "the irrationality of ζ(5) itself was still very open. On September 17, 2026, a few weeks after powerful AI tools became available, Fauzan posted online a proof that ζ(5) is irrational [7]. The principal idea was a new use of Hankel determinants." Their proof replaces the choice of one polynomial P_n by the Hankel determinant Δ_{n,m} = det(L_n(t^{i+j}))_{0≤i,j<m} of a linear functional built from the Lai–Yu rational functions; three ingredients: denominator bound (Pochhammer basis; "No sophisticated p-adic estimates are in use"), size bound, and a general non-vanishing result for Hankel matrices of signed measures on discrete sets. "our design of the linear functional L_n deviates significantly from its prototype from [7]. While using Hankel determinants in [7] gives one access to better arithmetic than by traditional techniques, the improvement in Theorem 1.1 … comes entirely from the analytic estimates of the determinants." — [arXiv:2611.16156, §1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.16156)
- Provenance statements in the paper: "Declaration of generative AI use. The initial proof was completely produced by ChatGPT (GPT-6 Astra, OpenAI). The authors have verified and simplified the argument, rewritten the exposition, and take full responsibility". "Declaration from natural intelligence": "all the ingredients for the methodology in use here have been created before advancements of AI tools. For example, the potentials of Hankel determinant constructions are discussed in [Zudilin, A determinantal approach to irrationality] and of their multivariate generalisations in [Brown, Mellin transforms…]; the deep connection and a different perspective of the methodology are offered in the framework of holonomicity criteria in [Calegari–Dimitrov–Tang]." — [arXiv:2611.16156, §1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.16156)
- Scope note: this is a "many odd zeta values" theorem (covered by another researcher); it is recorded here because it certifies how four specialists describe Fauzan's ζ(5) method and its antecedents. It does not itself re-prove the irrationality of ζ(5).

### Inferences
- Size of Fauzan's margin (my arithmetic from quoted constants): the real-side exponent is U ≈ −1.36700 per K² and the normalisation cost A_200 ≈ 1.34960 per K², so the net is ≈ −0.0174 per K² (= −27.8 per n² with K = 40n): the determinant's decay beats the cleared denominators by about 1.3% of either quantity, and the inner constant (6.4) holds with a margin of order 6·10^{−8}. The second formalization proves a slightly weaker net constant (−U = 1.367 > A_eff = 1.36 > achieved 1.345). The argument is therefore numerically tight, which makes the reported machine checks material to its credibility.
- What changed relative to Sections 1–5: (a) the comparison "decay versus denominators" is made on the n² scale for a determinant of size ≈ n, where determinant-level p-adic divisibility (rank and Vandermonde-type effects) is much better than entrywise bounds; (b) non-vanishing comes for free from positivity of a moment matrix; (c) the ζ(3)-elimination problem disappears because the entries are forms in 1 and ζ(5) only (built from Hurwitz-type pole evaluations j⁴(ζ(5) − H_j^{(5)}) rather than from weight-5 period integrals). This is my reading of Fauzan's §1–2 together with Lai's and Fischler–Lai–Sprang–Zudilin's descriptions.
- Lineage: the Hankel/positivity mechanism is Zudilin's (2017), with Krattenthaler–Rochev–Väänänen–Zudilin (2009) and Brown (2026) as relatives; Fauzan's own reference list does not cite these, while the follow-up papers by Lai and by Fischler–Lai–Sprang–Zudilin do. Whether Fauzan's construction is an instance of, or goes beyond, Zudilin's Proposition 2 is not stated in any retrieved source; structurally it differs (entries are moments of a fixed weight against a varying rational function, and denominators are controlled on the determinant, not through δ_0δ_2⋯δ_{2n−2}).
- Evidence weighting: the strongest independent evidence is the mo271 formalization (no extra axioms, benchmark statement copied from an external repository), followed by Romik's (PNT as axiom). Both are self-reported by README; I did not compile them. Expert reliance (Lai: "breakthrough"; Zudilin et al.: "a proof … posted online") is strong sociological evidence but not peer review. Wording in the final report should therefore be along the lines "a proof was announced in September 2026 and has reportedly been formally verified; journal refereeing was not yet documented on 15 Nov 2026".
