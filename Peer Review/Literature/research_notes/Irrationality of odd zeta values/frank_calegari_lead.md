## 1. Calegari–Dimitrov–Tang: "The linear independence of 1, ζ(2), and L(2,χ₋₃)" (2024), "The unbounded denominators conjecture" as background, and later joint papers (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
CDT prove L(2,χ₋₃) ∉ ℚ (indeed 1, π², L(2,χ₋₃) are ℚ-linearly independent) by a new "arithmetic holonomy bound" applied to Zagier's known Apéry-type sequences; they present it as the first new irrationality of an explicit L(k,χ) since Apéry's ζ(3). The paper proves nothing about the real ζ(5). The only ζ(5)-flavoured output of the method is the 2-adic statement ζ₂(5) ∉ ℚ (written up in their ICM paper, arXiv:2510.04156), and the authors say explicitly that it has no direct bearing on the classical ζ(5).

- **Abstract (verbatim).** "We prove the irrationality of the classical Dirichlet L-value L(2,χ₋₃). The argument applies a new kind of arithmetic holonomy bound to a well-known construction of Zagier. In fact our work also establishes the Q-linear independence of 1, ζ(2), and L(2,χ₋₃). We also give a number of other applications of our method to other problems in irrationality." [D] — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Theorem A (from the TeX source).** "The period L(2,χ₋₃) = Σ_{n≥0} ( 1/(3n+1)² − 1/(3n+2)² ) = 0.7813024128964862968… = ∬_{1≥y≥x≥0} dx dy / ( y(1+x+x²) ) = −∫₀¹ log(x) dx/(1+x+x²) is irrational. More generally, the three periods 1, π², L(2,χ₋₃) are linearly independent over Q." [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Corollary.** Irrationality of four trigamma-type sums, e.g. 1/1² + 1/4² + 1/7² + … = L(2,χ₋₃)/2 + 2π²/27; hence ψ₁(n/6) is irrational for every positive integer n. "These are the first new irrationality results for ψ₁ since Legendre's proof in 1794 that π² is irrational!" [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Theorem B (products of two logarithms).** For integers m, n ∉ {−1, 0} with |m/n − 1| < 10⁻⁶, log(1+1/m)·log(1+1/n) is irrational; for m ≠ n the numbers 1, log(1+1/m), log(1+1/n), log(1+1/m)log(1+1/n) are ℚ-linearly independent. [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Other by-product and disclaimers.** Irrationality of the Mahler measure of (1+x+y)⁴/3 ("which is not a canonical height"); the result has "no direct bearing on the conjectured irrationality of any such canonical heights" and "does not have any direct implications" for Thurston's question on rational relations among hyperbolic 3-manifold volumes (3√3·L(2,χ₋₃)/4 is the volume of the regular ideal tetrahedron). [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)

#### 1C. Method (what is new, what is reused)
- **No new approximating sequence.** "in this paper, we do *not* find (directly) any new convergent sequences to L(2,χ₋₃). Instead, we show how one can exploit the arithmetic nature of *known* approximations (found by Apéry and others) in a more subtle way using both methods from transcendental number theory and complex analysis." The sequences are those "(re-)discovered by Zagier" (§11 "Zagier's sequences A and C"). [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Apéry recast as an "Apéry limit".** Apéry's A(x), B(x) ∈ ℚ[[x]] satisfy a linear ODE with singularities at 0, ∞, (√2 ± 1)⁴; P(x) = B(x) − ζ(3)A(x) is the unique combination holomorphic at (√2 − 1)⁴; a_n ∈ ℤ, [1,…,n]³ b_n ∈ ℤ; irrationality follows from the inequality 4 log(√2+1) > 3. For other L-values with analogous A, B, P, "the analogous inequality … always seems to fail" (footnote: "except in one notable example found by Apéry himself with η = ζ(2)"). [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §1.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Key idea.** Exploit the analytic continuation of P(x) far beyond its disc of convergence, in the spirit of Borel–Pólya (integer power series on a domain of conformal radius > 1 are rational) and André's algebraicity criteria. Since Apéry-type series have denominators, "one should expect an *arithmetic holonomy* bound, where one bounds the dimension of a Q(x)-vector space generated by functions with certain denominator growth and analytic properties, and which is closed under differentiation." Assuming η ∈ ℚ yields more ℚ(x)-independent G-functions than the bound allows. [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §1.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Main bound (Theorem "basic main", paraphrased).** For m ℚ(x)-linearly independent f_i ∈ ℚ[[x]] with denominator types [1,…,b_{i,1}n]⋯[1,…,b_{i,r}n] and a holomorphic φ: (disc,0) → (ℂ,0) with |φ′(0)| > e^{σ_m} such that each f_i∘φ is meromorphic on |z| < 1, m is at most (a double integral of log|φ(z) − φ(w)|, or a rearrangement integral of log|φ|) divided by (log|φ′(0)| − τ(b)), where τ(b) = m⁻² Σ(2i−1)σ_i. Corollary (André; precursor Perelli–Zannier): such f is holonomic when |φ′(0)| > e^{b₁+…+b_r}. [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Tightness.** 14 functions are exhibited and the bound evaluates to "13.9938… < 14"; "Precisely 7 of our m = 14 functions are genuine". [D] — [arXiv:2510.04156 source, §6.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156)
- **Lineage.** The first holonomy bound (constant e) from the unbounded-denominators paper "is now far too crude to prove the irrationality of L(2,χ₋₃)"; Bost–Charles gave the finer double-integral bound for integer coefficients; in 2023 "Charles explained to us" its generalization; CDT add e ⇝ 2, the "Bost–Charles integral", convexity in Bost's slopes method, measure concentration, a Perelli–Zannier-style box principle. Calegari on his blog: "the m = 0 version of the holonomy theorem (version III) in this talk is a theorem in their [Bost–Charles] monograph" (arXiv:2206.14242). [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403); [Persiflage, 16 Jun 2024](https://galoisrepresentations.org/2024/06/16/a-talk-on-my-new-work-with-vesselin-dimitrov-and-yunqing-tang-on-irrationality/); [Bost–Charles, arXiv:2206.14242](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.14242)
- **Section titles (navigation).** 1 Introduction; 2 The main arithmetic holonomy bound; 3 Filtered evaluation modules and Functional Transcendence; 4 Concentration of measure; 5 The cost of an integration; 6 The fine holonomy bound; 7 Convexity in Bost's slopes method; 8 The finer holonomy bound with the Bost–Charles integral; 9 The relationship between Y(2) and …; 10 Pure functions on …; 11 Zagier's sequences A and C; 12 Functional Linear Independence; 13 Proof of the linear independence of …; 14 Products of two logarithms; 15 Complements and further questions; App. A Choosing a contour; App. B A dynamic box principle. [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Prerequisites, in Calegari's words.** "A number of people seemed keen on knowing the details of our paper up until the point they learnt it was 220 pages long. But I truly think that many of the ideas can be broken down into bite size chunks … The background for the lectures doesn't involve much beyond the complex theory of modular forms as well as some complex analysis." [D] — [Persiflage, "Arbeitsgemeinschaft 2026", 4 Jun 2025](https://galoisrepresentations.org/2025/06/04/arbeitsgemeinschaft-2026/)

#### 1D. What the paper says about ζ(5), other odd zeta values, and documented limitations
- **Real ζ(5): nothing proved, cited only as open** (footnote in 1B). [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **2-adic ζ(5) as the origin of the programme.** "We originally conceived of our new approach to irrationality in 2020, starting with an easy proof of the irrationality of the 2-adic avatar of ζ(5) (which now we finally exposit in a companion paper [zeta5]), and even during that year we realized with the help of [Zagier] that the method could apply in principle to L(2,χ₋₃). However, at the time, the holonomy bounds that we could prove following [André] were totally insufficient". The .bbl lists the companion as "Arithmetic holonomy bounds and the irrationality of the 2-adic ζ(5), 2024" without identifier. [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §15 and .bbl](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Brown–Zudilin's ζ(5) approximations do not feed the method.** "in many of the more intricate ones in the literature — such as in Zudilin's work … on simultaneous approximation to ζ(2) and ζ(3), and in Brown and Zudilin's work … on ζ(5) — progress towards a not-yet-attained irrationality goal is measured by setting up a complex set of parameters to maximize the *worthiness exponent* limsup{ −log|η − p/q| / log q }. The latter is very far from faithfully measuring worthiness as a potential for applying in our framework of rational holonomy bounds." [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §15](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Catalan's constant G = L(2,χ₋₄) is out of reach with known inputs.** "Since e² > 16·(1/4) and e⁴ > ((1+√5)/2)⁵ (by a wide margin!), this definitely precludes an approach to the irrationality of the Catalan constant by our method using either of these particular families of rational approximants, unless some completely new idea is discovered." [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §11](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Denominator type [1,…,n]³ on ℙ¹∖{0,1,∞} is beyond the method.** "Since 16 < e^τ = e³, our methods have nothing directly to say about [the finite-dimensionality question]; we do not even have a guess as to what the answer might be." [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §15 "The structure ring"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Other stated gaps.** For the [1,…,n]² layer on ℙ¹∖{0,1,∞} "the best bound on the dimension we can currently establish is 9 rather than 5 … Ruling any possible further functions out remains a difficult problem currently beyond the reach of our methods in this paper." Update from Calegari's blog (9 Sep 2026): using ChatGPT 5.6 to optimize the auxiliary map ψ gave improvements "not quite good enough (yet) to simplify our proof of the irrationality of L(2,χ₋₃) in any meaningful way, but … good enough to prove that the Q(x)-vector space generated by functions f(x) ∈ Q[[x]] on P¹∖{0,1,∞} which have denominator type [1,2,…,n]² has dimension at most 8 rather than dimension at most 9. (We still suspect the actual answer is 5.)" (blog statement; no paper). [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403); [Persiflage, "Look, Mom, I pressed a button! (Go edition)", 9 Sep 2026](https://galoisrepresentations.org/2026/09/09/look-mom-i-pressed-a-button-go-edition/)

With the Hauptmodul x = Δ(2τ)/Δ(τ) of X₀(2), suppose ζ₂(2k+1) ∈ ℚ; then H := E*_{2k}·E*_{−2k} is an overconvergent modular function with rational coefficients; Buzzard's theorem gives 2-adic radius 2¹²; the Archimedean radius is 1/64 but H(x(q)) is analytic on |q| < 1; by Kontsevich–Zagier H satisfies an inhomogeneous linear ODE of exact order 5 = 2k+1, giving six ℚ(x)-independent functions, while the holonomy bound gives "4.43206… < 6". "For k = 1, the usual Apéry argument gives that ζ₂(3) ∉ Q … For k = 2, it is crucial that we exploit the analytic continuation of H(x) to a region beyond this disc." A cruder 2020-era bound already gives "5.52667… < 6". [D] — [arXiv:2510.04156 source, §6.2 and Remark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156)
- **Relation to Lai–Sprang–Zudilin (a genuinely different construction).** "Lai, Sprang, and Zudilin have found a completely new proof of the irrationality of ζ₂(5), more in the spirit of Apéry's original argument, by working with a differential equation with better convergence properties. The corresponding local system in their case appears to have a genuinely different geometric origin, coming from a moduli space of Calabi–Yau 3-folds rather than a symmetric power of the Picard–Fuchs equation of a modular curve." [D] — [arXiv:2510.04156 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156)
- **Disclaimer on the real ζ(5).** "Neither of these results, however, have any direct bearing on the (ir)rationality of the classical ζ(5)." (Heuristic explanation quoted in §5.) [D] — [arXiv:2510.04156 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156)

### Inferences
- The CDT method strengthens the Apéry-limit framework rather than supplying new approximations: it turns "linear forms do not decay fast enough" into "too many independent G-functions with controlled denominators on a large conformal domain". It needs as input a low-order ODE (motivic local system) in which the target constant appears alone or separably. For ζ(5) ∈ ℝ the authors know no such input (§5).
- The obstruction is quantitative: available conformal size must beat e^τ (τ the denominator rate). Already for [1,…,n]³ on ℙ¹∖{0,1,∞} this fails (16 < e³); ζ(5)-type constructions would carry worse denominator rates (this last clause is my extrapolation, not a statement in the paper).
- The CDT results and the September 2026 ζ(5) claim use different mechanisms (holonomy rank bounds vs. arithmetic gains in Hankel determinants), so neither supersedes the other.

## 2. Calegari, "Irrationality of certain p-adic periods for small p" (IMRN 2005) and Beukers' related p-adic work

### Takeaway
The 2005 paper carries Beukers' *modular* proof of Apéry's theorem into the p-adic world via overconvergent Eisenstein series, proving ζ₂(3), ζ₃(3) and the 2-adic Catalan constant irrational; the argument provably fails for p = 5, 7, 13. Beukers re-proved and extended these by Stieltjes continued fractions. These concern p-adic numbers, not real odd zeta values, but they are the seed of the CDT ζ₂(5) theorem.

### Cited Findings

#### 2A. Resource card: Calegari 2005

## 5. Calegari's statements on why ζ(5) is hard and what would be needed

### Takeaway
The fullest statement is a footnote in the CDT ICM paper (Oct 2025): an Apéry-type scheme needs a motivic local system (ODE) in which the L-value appears as a period on its own; for real odd zeta values periods get "mixed" (notably with powers of π), and "we know of no simple analogous construction for ζ(2k+1) ∈ ℝ", whereas p-adically ζ_p(2) = 0 and an ODE for ζ₂(2k+1) exists for every k. After the 2026 ζ(5) proof his position is that the new method's ideas are not yet understood and that "Apéry's result from almost 50 years ago is still something we don't really understand."

### Cited Findings
- **"No direct bearing".** "Neither of these results [CDT's and Lai–Sprang–Zudilin's proofs of ζ₂(5) ∉ ℚ], however, have any direct bearing on the (ir)rationality of the classical ζ(5)." [D] — [arXiv:2510.04156 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156) (5 Oct 2025)
- **The heuristic (footnote, verbatim extracts).** "One may wonder why one can prove the irrationality of ζ₂(5) ∈ Q₂ but not the irrationality of ζ(5) ∈ R. We give a heuristic explanation why the first problem is easier." … "A consequence of this conjecture [Grothendieck's period conjecture for mixed Tate motives over Z] is that the Q-algebra generated by ζ(2) and the odd values ζ(2k+1) for k ≥ 1 is free on these generators; equivalently, that these values are all transcendental and moreover algebraically independent." … "the p-adic comparison theorem naturally places the p-adic analogue of 2πi as an element in B_dR which maps to zero in C_p. In particular, the only sensible definition of ζ_p(2) returns ζ_p(2) = 0". … "The first step in any Apéry type scheme is to produce a motivic local system where the corresponding L-value arises as a period. ### Inferences
- In CDT's framework a proof of ζ(5) ∉ ℚ "of Apéry-limit type" would need: (i) a motivic local system/ODE where ζ(5) is an Apéry limit uncontaminated by ζ(3), ζ(2)ζ(3), π⁴, …; (ii) solutions whose denominator rate τ is beaten by the conformal size of an available analytic map; (iii) enough explicit ℚ(x)-independent functions to exceed the holonomy bound if ζ(5) ∈ ℚ. The authors report (i) as missing. This three-item list is my synthesis, not theirs.
- Brown–Zudilin's M_{0,8} construction is an instance of "mixing" (ζ(3) and ζ(5) together), which is why the authors cite it when explaining the difficulty.
- The 2026 Hankel-determinant proof attacks a different axis (determinant-level arithmetic gains for classical linear forms in 1 and ζ(5)); it sidesteps the "missing ODE" diagnosis without contradicting it. Calegari has not, in the retrieved posts, related the new method to holonomy bounds or G-function theory, and he has not explained the k ≤ 5 limit.
