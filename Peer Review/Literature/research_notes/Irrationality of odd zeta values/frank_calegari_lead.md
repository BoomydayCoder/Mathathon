## 1. Calegari–Dimitrov–Tang: "The linear independence of 1, ζ(2), and L(2,χ₋₃)" (2024), "The unbounded denominators conjecture" as background, and later joint papers (2025–2026)

### Takeaway
CDT prove L(2,χ₋₃) ∉ ℚ (indeed 1, π², L(2,χ₋₃) are ℚ-linearly independent) by a new "arithmetic holonomy bound" applied to Zagier's known Apéry-type sequences; they describe it as the first new irrationality of an explicit L(k,χ) since Apéry's ζ(3). The paper proves nothing about the real ζ(5); the only ζ(5)-flavoured output of the method is the 2-adic ζ₂(5) ∉ ℚ (proved in their 2025 ICM paper, arXiv:2510.04156), and the authors say explicitly that this has no direct bearing on the classical ζ(5).

- **Type / status.** Research preprint, submitted to a journal (per the arXiv comment), not shown as published. Status: *accepted proof in the community sense* (subject of a Séminaire Bourbaki exposé, a Quanta feature, and independent follow-up work — see §4), *formal journal publication not confirmed*. [D] — [arXiv:2510.04156 source, bibliography](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156); [Lai, arXiv:2610.06838 source, bibliography](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06838)
- **Abstract (verbatim).** "We prove the irrationality of the classical Dirichlet L-value L(2,χ₋₃). The argument applies a new kind of arithmetic holonomy bound to a well-known construction of Zagier. In fact our work also establishes the Q-linear independence of 1, ζ(2), and L(2,χ₋₃). We also give a number of other applications of our method to other problems in irrationality." [D] — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Theorem A (verbatim from the TeX source, formulas transliterated).** "The period L(2,χ₋₃) = Σ_{n≥0} ( 1/(3n+1)² − 1/(3n+2)² ) = 0.7813024128964862968… = ∬_{1≥y≥x≥0} dx dy / ( y(1+x+x²) ) = −∫₀¹ log(x) dx/(1+x+x²) is irrational. More generally, the three periods 1, π², L(2,χ₋₃) are linearly independent over Q." [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Corollary.** Irrationality of four trigamma-type sums, e.g. 1/1² + 1/4² + 1/7² + … = L(2,χ₋₃)/2 + 2π²/27; consequently ψ₁(n/6) is irrational for every positive integer n. The authors write: "These are the first new irrationality results for ψ₁ since Legendre's proof in 1794 that π² is irrational!" [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Theorem B (products of two logarithms).** For integers m, n ∉ {−1, 0} with |m/n − 1| < 10⁻⁶, log(1+1/m)·log(1+1/n) is irrational; moreover for m ≠ n the four numbers 1, log(1+1/m), log(1+1/n), log(1+1/m)log(1+1/n) are ℚ-linearly independent. [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Other stated by-product.** Irrationality of the Mahler measure of the rational-coefficient polynomial (1+x+y)⁴/3 ("which is not a canonical height"); the authors stress that the result has "no direct bearing on the conjectured irrationality of any such canonical heights" and "does not have any direct implications" for Thurston's question on rational relations among hyperbolic 3-manifold volumes. [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)

#### 1B. Why it is regarded as the first new Apéry-type result in decades
- The authors' own framing: "in the (approximately) last 140 years since [Weierstrass] only a *single* further explicit number L(k,χ) has been shown to be irrational, namely Apéry's unexpected 1978 proof that ζ(3) is irrational. In this paper, we establish the irrationality of a new L-value L(k,χ); in some sense the 'simplest' open case corresponding to k = 2 and the character χ = χ₋₃ of smallest possible conductor". [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §1.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- On the state of the art after Apéry: "Ever since Apéry's result, considerable effort has been expended in searching for analogous sequences which demonstrate the irrationality of other L-values L(k,χ) … Unfortunately, despite enormous efforts, no such sequences have ever been found." The attached footnote records the family results: "the theorem of Ball and Rivoal … that infinitely many odd zeta values are irrational. One refinement by Zudilin … proves that at least one of the values ζ(5), ζ(7), ζ(9) and ζ(11) is irrational." This footnote is the only place the real ζ(5) appears in the introduction. [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §1.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- Independent expert framing two years later: Li Lai writes "Before the AI age, a remarkable result was achieved by Calegari, Dimitrov, and Y. Tang", stating their theorem alongside Apéry and Ball–Rivoal as one of three landmark theorems. [D] — [Lai, arXiv:2610.06838 source, §1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06838) (v1 5 Oct 2026, v2 15 Oct 2026)

#### 1C. Method (what is new, what is reused)
- **No new approximating sequence.** "in this paper, we do *not* find (directly) any new convergent sequences to L(2,χ₋₃). Instead, we show how one can exploit the arithmetic nature of *known* approximations (found by Apéry and others) in a more subtle way using both methods from transcendental number theory and complex analysis." The sequences are those "(re-)discovered by Zagier" (Zagier's cases A and C; §11 of the paper is titled "Zagier's sequences A and C"). [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Apéry reformulated as an "Apéry limit".** Apéry's generating functions A(x), B(x) ∈ ℚ[[x]] satisfy a linear ODE with singularities at 0, ∞, (√2 ± 1)⁴; P(x) = B(x) − ζ(3)A(x) is the unique combination that is holomorphic at the nearer singularity (√2 − 1)⁴; denominators are a_n ∈ ℤ, [1,…,n]³ b_n ∈ ℤ; irrationality follows from the numerical inequality 4 log(√2+1) > 3. CDT: for other L-values with analogous A, B, P, "the analogous inequality … always seems to fail" (footnote: "except in one notable example found by Apéry himself with η = ζ(2)"). [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §1.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Key idea.** Use that P(x) continues analytically far beyond its disc of convergence (to a slit plane, or to the universal cover of ℙ¹ minus the singular points), in the spirit of Borel–Pólya (integer power series on a domain of conformal radius > 1 are rational) and André's algebraicity criteria. Because Apéry-type series have denominators, algebraicity is the wrong target; instead "one should expect an *arithmetic holonomy* bound, where one bounds the dimension of a Q(x)-vector space generated by functions with certain denominator growth and analytic properties, and which is closed under differentiation." Assuming η ∈ ℚ produces more ℚ(x)-linearly independent G-functions with the prescribed denominator type and analytic continuation than the bound permits. [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §1.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Main bound (Theorem 2.x "basic main", paraphrased from the source).** For m ℚ(x)-linearly independent f_i ∈ ℚ[[x]] with denominator types [1,…,b_{i,1}n]⋯[1,…,b_{i,r}n] and a holomorphic φ: (disc, 0) → (ℂ, 0) with |φ′(0)| > e^{σ_m} such that every f_i∘φ is meromorphic on the unit disc, m is bounded by (a double integral of log|φ(z) − φ(w)| or a rearrangement integral of log|φ|) divided by (log|φ′(0)| − τ(b)), where τ(b) = m⁻² Σ (2i−1)σ_i is a weighted denominator rate. A qualitative corollary (attributed to André, with a Perelli–Zannier precursor) says such f is holonomic. [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **How tight the numbers are.** In the L(2,χ₋₃) proof, 14 functions are exhibited and the bound evaluates to "13.9938… < 14"; "Precisely 7 of our m = 14 functions are genuine". [D] — [arXiv:2510.04156 source, §6.1, citing L2chi A.5.1 and Lemma 12.1.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156)
- **Lineage of the bound.** The first version of the holonomy bound (with constant e) came from the unbounded-denominators paper and "is now far too crude to prove the irrationality of L(2,χ₋₃)"; Bost–Charles gave the finer double-integral bound in the denominator-free case and "Charles explained to us" in 2023 how to generalize it; CDT then prove refinements (constant e ⇝ 2, a "Bost–Charles integral", convexity in Bost's slopes method, measure concentration in high-dimensional evaluation modules, a Perelli–Zannier-style "dynamic box principle"). "The whole § … is due to Fedja Nazarov." [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §§2, 16](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Table of contents (section titles from the source)**, useful for navigation: 1 Introduction; 2 The main arithmetic holonomy bound; 3 Filtered evaluation modules and Functional Transcendence; 4 Concentration of measure; 5 The cost of an integration; 6 The fine holonomy bound; 7 Convexity in Bost's slopes method; 8 The finer holonomy bound with the Bost–Charles integral; 9 The relationship between Y(2) and …; 10 Pure functions on …; 11 Zagier's sequences A and C; 12 Functional Linear Independence; 13 Proof of the linear independence of …; 14 Products of two logarithms; 15 Complements and further questions; Appendix A Choosing a contour; Appendix B A dynamic box principle. Prerequisites implied: G-functions (Siegel, André, Dwork), Arakelov/slopes method (Bost), modular curves Y(2)/Picard–Fuchs equations, potential theory/conformal mapping. [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)

#### 1D. What the paper says about ζ(5), other odd zeta values, and documented limitations
- **Real ζ(5): nothing proved, only cited as open.** See the footnote in 1B. [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **2-adic ζ(5): the origin of the whole programme.** "We originally conceived of our new approach to irrationality in 2020, starting with an easy proof of the irrationality of the 2-adic avatar of ζ(5) (which now we finally exposit in a companion paper [zeta5]), and even during that year we realized with the help of [Zagier] that the method could apply in principle to L(2,χ₋₃). However, at the time, the holonomy bounds that we could prove following [André] were totally insufficient". The bibliography lists the companion as "Arithmetic holonomy bounds and the irrationality of the 2-adic ζ(5), 2024" with no identifier. [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §15 "A historical note and acknowledgments" and .bbl](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Brown–Zudilin's ζ(5) approximations do not feed the method.** "in many of the more intricate ones in the literature — such as in Zudilin's work … on simultaneous approximation to ζ(2) and ζ(3), and in Brown and Zudilin's work … on ζ(5) — progress towards a not-yet-attained irrationality goal is measured by setting up a complex set of parameters to maximize the *worthiness exponent* limsup{ −log|η − p/q| / log q }. The latter is very far from faithfully measuring worthiness as a potential for applying in our framework of rational holonomy bounds." [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §15](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Catalan's constant G = L(2,χ₋₄) is out of reach of the method as it stands.** After examining Zudilin/Rivoal/Nesterenko-type approximants and Zagier's case E: "Since e² > 16·(1/4) and e⁴ > ((1+√5)/2)⁵ (by a wide margin!), this definitely precludes an approach to the irrationality of the Catalan constant by our method using either of these particular families of rational approximants, unless some completely new idea is discovered." [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §11](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Denominator type [1,…,n]³ on ℙ¹∖{0,1,∞} is beyond the method.** Question: is the ℚ(x)-space generated by geometric-origin G-functions on ℙ¹∖{0,1,∞} with denominators [1,…,n]³ finite-dimensional? "Since 16 < e^τ = e³, our methods have nothing directly to say about [this question]; we do not even have a guess as to what the answer might be." (The conformal size of the universal covering map λ is 16; the criterion needs it to exceed e^τ.) [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §15 "The structure ring"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Other stated gaps.** For the [1,…,n]² layer on ℙ¹∖{0,1,∞} "the best bound on the dimension we can currently establish is 9 rather than 5 … Ruling any possible further functions out remains a difficult problem currently beyond the reach of our methods in this paper." The log-product theorem needs |1 − m/n| < 10⁻⁶. The explicit holonomy bounds are, in the authors' words, akin to "Thue's paradigm with its organic ineffectivity" (effectivity is addressed only in the 2025 sequel). [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)

- **Title / authors / date / status.** "Arithmetic holonomy bounds and effective Diophantine approximation", Frank Calegari, Vesselin Dimitrov, Yunqing Tang, arXiv:2510.04156 [math.NT], v1 5 Oct 2025. arXiv comment: "This is our submission for the (ICM) Proceedings 2026". [D] — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156), [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.04156)

[D] — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156)
- **Theorem (L(2,χ₋₃) measure).** "For all p/q ∈ Q except possibly a finite and computable list, we have |L(2,χ₋₃) − p/q| > 1/q^{24781}." Same for any ℚ-linear combination of L(2,χ₋₃) and π². [D] — [arXiv:2510.04156 source, §6.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156)
- **Theorem (2-adic ζ(5)) — a result about ζ₂(5) ∈ ℚ₂, not about ζ(5) ∈ ℝ.** "For sufficiently large p or q, we have |ζ₂(5) − p/q|₂ > 1/max(|p|,|q|)^{20}." In particular ζ₂(5) ∉ ℚ, "which in early 2020 had been our first joint result in this collaboration." [D] — [arXiv:2510.04156 source, §6.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156)
- **Method for ζ₂(5).** Start "with the same setup as [Calegari 2005], where it is proved that ζ₂(3) ∉ Q. The new input is to consider a non-trivial template φ(z) at the Archimedean place." With the Hauptmodul x = Δ(2τ)/Δ(τ) of X₀(2), assume ζ₂(2k+1) ∈ ℚ; then H := E*_{2k}·E*_{−2k} (product of the classical 2-stabilized Eisenstein series and the overconvergent weight −2k Eisenstein series whose constant term is ζ₂(1+2k)/2) is an overconvergent modular function with rational coefficients; Buzzard's theorem gives 2-adic radius 2¹²; the Archimedean radius is 1/64 but H(x(q)) is analytic on |q| < 1; by Kontsevich–Zagier H satisfies an inhomogeneous linear ODE of exact order 5 = 2k+1, giving six ℚ(x)-independent functions, while the holonomy bound gives "4.43206… < 6". "For k = 1, the usual Apéry argument gives that ζ₂(3) ∉ Q, and this is essentially the content of [Calegari 2005]. For k = 2, it is crucial that we exploit the analytic continuation of H(x) to a region beyond this disc." A cruder 2020-era bound already gives "5.52667… < 6". [D] — [arXiv:2510.04156 source, §6.2 and Remark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156)
- **Relation to Lai–Sprang–Zudilin.** "Quite recently, Lai, Sprang, and Zudilin have found a completely new proof of the irrationality of ζ₂(5), more in the spirit of Apéry's original argument, by working with a differential equation with better convergence properties. The corresponding local system in their case appears to have a genuinely different geometric origin, coming from a moduli space of Calabi–Yau 3-folds rather than a symmetric power of the Picard–Fuchs equation of a modular curve." (So: two genuinely different constructions for the same 2-adic constant.) [D] — [arXiv:2510.04156 source, Remark after §6.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156)
- **Explicit disclaimer on the real ζ(5).** "Neither of these results, however, have any direct bearing on the (ir)rationality of the classical ζ(5)." (Full heuristic explanation quoted in §5 below.) [D] — [arXiv:2510.04156 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156)

### Inferences
- The CDT method is a *reformulation and strengthening of the Apéry-limit framework*, not a new source of approximations: it converts "the linear forms do not decay fast enough" into "too many independent G-functions with controlled denominators on a large conformal domain". It therefore needs, as input, a low-order ODE (motivic local system) in which the target constant appears alone, or mixed only with constants one can separate. For ζ(5) ∈ ℝ no such input is known to the authors (see §5).
- Their documented obstruction is quantitative and transparent: the conformal size available must beat e^τ where τ is the denominator rate. For ζ(3)-type denominators [1,…,n]³ on ℙ¹∖{0,1,∞} this already fails (16 < e³), and ζ(5)-type constructions would naturally carry denominators of type [1,…,n]⁵, i.e. a far worse τ (this last clause is my extrapolation, not a statement in the paper).
- Nothing in the CDT papers contradicts or is superseded by the September 2026 ζ(5) claim; the two lines of work use different mechanisms (holonomy rank bounds vs. p-adic gains in Hankel determinants of linear forms).

## 2. Calegari, "Irrationality of certain p-adic periods for small p" (IMRN 2005) and Beukers' related p-adic work

### Takeaway
Calegari's 2005 paper transports Beukers' *modular* proof of Apéry's theorem to the p-adic setting via overconvergent Eisenstein series, proving ζ₂(3), ζ₃(3) and the 2-adic Catalan constant L₂(2,χ₄) irrational; the same argument provably fails for p = 5, 7, 13. Beukers then re-proved and extended these by classical Stieltjes continued fractions. These are results about p-adic numbers; they say nothing about real odd zeta values, but they are the seed of the CDT ζ₂(5) theorem.

### Cited Findings

#### 2A. Resource card — Calegari 2005
- **Title / author / date / status.** "Irrationality of certain p-adic periods for small p", Frank Calegari. arXiv:math/0408214 [math.NT], 16 Aug 2004 (single version; comment "Preprint"). Published: Int. Math. Res. Not. (2005), pp. 1235–1249 (as cited by CDT in 2025). Status: *accepted proof, published*; about ζ_p(3) and a p-adic Catalan constant, not ζ(5). [D] — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0408214), [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0408214); journal data from [arXiv:2510.04156 source, bibliography](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156)
- **Abstract (verbatim).** "Following Apery's proof of the irrationality of zeta(3), Beukers found an elegant reinterpretation of Apery's arguments using modular forms. We show how Beukers arguments can be adapted to a p-adic setting. In this context, certain functional equations arising from Eichler integrals are replaced by the notion of overconvergent p-adic modular forms, and the periods themselves arise not as coefficients of period polynomials but as constant terms of p-adic Eisenstein series. We prove that the analogue of zeta(3) is irrational for p = 2 and 3, as well as the 2-adic analogue of Catalan's constant." [D] — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0408214)
- **Theorems (from the TeX source).** "If p=2 then ζ_p(3) ∉ Q." "If p=3 then ζ_p(3) ∉ Q." For the character χ of conductor 4: there are integers p_n, q_n with q_n → ∞ such that, with θ = 8 log 2/(4 log 2 + 2) = 1.1618804316… > 1, 0 < ‖L₂(2,χ) − p_n/q_n‖₂ ≤ max{|p_n|,|q_n|}^{−(θ−ε)}; "In particular, L₂(2,χ) is irrational." The approximants satisfy the Apéry-like recurrence (n+1)² u_{n+1} = (4 − 32n²) u_n − 256(n−1)² u_{n−1}. [D] — [arXiv:math/0408214 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0408214)
- **Method.** p-adic irrationality criterion (rational approximations p_n/q_n with ‖η − p_n/q_n‖_p ≤ max{|p_n|,|q_n|}^{−1−δ}); ζ_p(1+2n)/2 realized as the constant term of an overconvergent Eisenstein series of negative weight on Γ₀(p) (Coleman's theory); Kubota–Leopoldt values ζ_p(3) = L_p(3, id). Opening sentence, useful as historical context: "Apéry's proof of the irrationality of ζ(3) is now over 25 years old, and it is perhaps surprising that his methods have not yielded any significantly new results (although further progress has been made on the irrationality of zeta values [Ball–Rivoal], [Zudilin])." [D] — [arXiv:math/0408214 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0408214)
- **Documented limitation.** "Although the proof succeeds for p=3, it fails for the other primes where X₀(p) has genus zero (p=5, 7 and 13). For example, for p=5 we find that θ = 3/(3/2 + 3/log 5) = 0.8917942081 < 1." The paper treats only ζ_p(3) (k = 1); CDT later remark that the construction "directly generalizes to produce an ODE over P¹∖{0, −1/2⁶, ∞} associated to ζ₂(2k+1) for any k", with ζ₂(5) needing the extra holonomy-bound input. [D] — [arXiv:math/0408214 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0408214); [arXiv:2510.04156 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156)
- **ζ(5) in this paper.** Appears only in the bibliography (Zudilin's "One of the numbers ζ(5), ζ(7), ζ(9), ζ(11) is irrational"). [D] — [arXiv:math/0408214 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0408214)

#### 2B. Resource card — Beukers' p-adic paper
- **Title / author / date.** "Irrationality of some p-adic L-values", F. Beukers. arXiv:math/0603277 [math.NT], v1 12 Mar 2006, v2 20 Jun 2006. [D] — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0603277), [arXiv PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0603277)
- **Abstract (verbatim).** "We give a proof of the irrationality of the p-adic zeta-values ζ_p(k) for p=2,3 and k=2,3. Such results were recently obtained by F.Calegari as an application of overconvergent p-adic modular forms. In this paper we present an approach using classical continued fractions discovered by Stieltjes. In addition we show irrationality of some other p-adic L-series values, and values of the p-adic Hurwitz zeta-function." [D] — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0603277)

[D] for the abstract — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0603277); [S] for the Persiflage link — [Persiflage, 24 Sep 2026](https://galoisrepresentations.org/2026/09/24/zeta5-is-irrational/)

[D] — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23088); [Fauzan preprint PDF, ref. 8](https://zenodo.org/records/22826419)

### Inferences
- The 2005 paper is the origin of the specific geometric input (Eisenstein series on X₀(2)) that CDT reuse for ζ₂(5); what was missing in 2005 for weight 5 was an inequality, later supplied by holonomy bounds. This is a clean example of the CDT philosophy: same approximations, stronger arithmetic criterion.
- Because Calegari's 2005 argument fails for p = 5 (θ ≈ 0.89 < 1), ζ₅(3) remained open by that method; this is the constant whose 2026 AI-assisted proofs reportedly prompted his 5 Sep 2026 blog post (see §3).

### Gaps
- IMRN issue number and DOI for the 2005 paper not retrieved (publisher site blocked); page range is from CDT's bibliography. [M]: issue no. 20.
- Beukers' abstract speaks of "ζ_p(k) for p=2,3 and k=2,3", whereas CDT note that in the Kubota–Leopoldt normalization "the only sensible definition of ζ_p(2) returns ζ_p(2) = 0". The two statements use different conventions (Beukers' even-argument values are presumably twisted L-values); I did not open Beukers' full text to pin down his normalization. Publication venue of Beukers' paper not retrieved ([M]: Acta Math. Sinica (Engl. Ser.) 24 (2008)).

## 5. Calegari's statements on why ζ(5) is hard and what would be needed

### Takeaway
The clearest statement is a footnote in the CDT ICM paper (Oct 2025): Apéry-type schemes need a motivic local system (an ODE) in which the target L-value appears as a period by itself; for real odd zeta values the periods get "mixed" (notably with powers of π), and "we know of no simple analogous construction for ζ(2k+1) ∈ ℝ", whereas p-adically ζ_p(2) = 0 and an ODE for ζ₂(2k+1) exists for every k. Combined with the 2024 paper's quantitative criteria, the documented requirement is: a suitable low-order ODE with small denominator type and large conformal size.

### Cited Findings
- **"No direct bearing".** After describing their own and Lai–Sprang–Zudilin's proofs of ζ₂(5) ∉ ℚ: "Neither of these results, however, have any direct bearing on the (ir)rationality of the classical ζ(5)." [D] — [arXiv:2510.04156 source, Remark after Theorem on ζ₂(5)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156) (5 Oct 2025)
- **The heuristic, verbatim (footnote).** "One may wonder why one can prove the irrationality of ζ₂(5) ∈ Q₂ but not the irrationality of ζ(5) ∈ R. We give a heuristic explanation why the first problem is easier." … "A consequence of this conjecture [Grothendieck's period conjecture for mixed Tate motives over Z] is that the Q-algebra generated by ζ(2) and the odd values ζ(2k+1) for k ≥ 1 is free on these generators; equivalently, that these values are all transcendental and moreover algebraically independent." … "One of the most basic manifestations of this difference is that the p-adic comparison theorem naturally places the p-adic analogue of 2πi as an element in B_dR which maps to zero in C_p. In particular, the only sensible definition of ζ_p(2) returns ζ_p(2) = 0". … "The first step in any Apéry type scheme is to produce a motivic local system where the corresponding L-value arises as a period. One issue, when trying to construct such local systems is that various periods get 'mixed' together and it is hard to separate the period one is particularly interested in. (One interesting discussion of these issues from a geometric point of view can be found in [Brown–Zudilin].) The point is now that, p-adically, the vanishing of ζ_p(2) makes it easier to realize the odd zeta values on their own. Relevantly for us, the construction in [Calegari 2005] directly generalizes to produce an ODE over P¹ ∖ {0, −1/2⁶, ∞} associated to ζ₂(2k+1) for any k; we know of no simple analogous construction for ζ(2k+1) ∈ R." [D] — [arXiv:2510.04156 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156)
- **Worked illustration with Catalan's constant (same footnote).** For the hypergeometric-type operator L = x(1+16x)² d²/dx² + (1+16x)² d/dx − 4, the real identity is D(x) = B(x) − (G/2)·A(x) − (π²/16)·C(x) while the 2-adic identity is D(x) = B(x) − (G₂/2)·A(x); "the 'same' equation … as long as we interpret the 2-adic meaning of (2πi)² to be zero, but Apéry's method only allows one to deduce that G₂ ∈ Q₂ ∖ Q, but not that G ∈ R ∖ Q." [D] — [arXiv:2510.04156 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156)
- **Counter-caveat on the p-adic side.** "we can't as yet even rule out the possibility that ζ_p(3) = 0 for all sufficiently large p, let alone prove that all those values are irrational." [D] — [arXiv:2510.04156 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04156)
- **Why Apéry's proof is a numerical accident (2024).** Irrationality of ζ(3) rests on "the inequality 4 log(√2+1) > 3"; for other L-values with analogous constructions "the analogous inequality … always seems to fail … and one can draw no consequences about the arithmetic of the corresponding L-value." CDT's remedy is to use the full analytic continuation (conformal size) and the ODE structure rather than just the radius of convergence. [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §1.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Quantitative requirement of the holonomy method.** A mapping φ with conformal size |φ′(0)| > e^{b₁+…+b_r} (denominator type [1,…,b₁n]⋯[1,…,b_r n]) is needed even to conclude holonomicity; and for [1,…,n]³ on ℙ¹∖{0,1,∞}, "Since 16 < e^τ = e³, our methods have nothing directly to say". [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, Corollary "holonomic criterion" and §15](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)
- **Existing ζ(5) approximations are measured on the wrong scale for this method.** Brown–Zudilin's cellular approximations optimize a "worthiness exponent" that "is very far from faithfully measuring worthiness as a potential for applying in our framework of rational holonomy bounds." (Brown–Zudilin, arXiv:2210.03391, v3 29 Jan 2026: effective approximations with 0 < |ζ(5) − p/q| < q^{−0.86}, from cellular integrals on M_{0,8} giving simultaneous approximations to ζ(3) and ζ(5).) [D] — [arXiv:2408.15403 source, §15](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403); [arXiv:2210.03391](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03391)
- **The 2005 formulation of the same puzzle.** "Apéry's proof of the irrationality of ζ(3) is now over 25 years old, and it is perhaps surprising that his methods have not yielded any significantly new results". [D] — [arXiv:math/0408214 source](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0408214) (16 Aug 2004)
- **2026 blog position (provisional wording).** He wants to know not only that ζ(5) is irrational but why; and in the 24 Sep 2026 post the Hankel/Padé scheme reaches k = 2, 3, 4, 5 "but not (at least directly) for larger k". [S] — [Persiflage, 5 Sep 2026](https://galoisrepresentations.org/2026/09/05/look-mom-i-pressed-a-button/); [Persiflage, 24 Sep 2026](https://galoisrepresentations.org/2026/09/24/zeta5-is-irrational/)

### Inferences
- In CDT's framework, a proof of ζ(5) ∉ ℚ "of Apéry-limit type" would need: (i) a motivic local system/ODE over a punctured ℙ¹ (or a curve with good uniformization) where ζ(5) appears as an Apéry limit without contamination by ζ(3), ζ(2)ζ(3), π⁴ etc.; (ii) solutions with a denominator type whose rate τ is beaten by the conformal size of an available analytic map; (iii) enough explicit ℚ(x)-independent functions to exceed the holonomy bound under the assumption ζ(5) ∈ ℚ. The authors report (i) as missing. These three conditions are my synthesis of the cited passages, not a list stated by the authors.
- Brown–Zudilin's M_{0,8} construction is, in this language, an example of "mixing" (ζ(3) and ζ(5) appear together), which is why the authors point to it when explaining the difficulty.
- The 2026 Hankel-determinant claim attacks the problem on a different axis (gaining arithmetic from determinants of many classical linear forms in 1 and ζ(5), per Lai's summary), so it neither uses nor contradicts the "missing ODE" diagnosis; whether it supplies the "why" Calegari asks for is, per the blog excerpts, part of what his 24 Sep post tries to work out via Prévost.
