## 1. Apéry's original approach (1978–1981): the recurrence, the Apéry numbers, denominators d_n³ and the rate (1+√2)⁴

### Takeaway
Apéry's proof rests on two rational solutions of one three-term recurrence whose ratio converges to ζ(3) at geometric rate (√2−1)⁸ while denominators grow only like d_n³ ≈ e^{3n}; the "miracle" e³(√2−1)⁴ < 1 is exactly what fails in every literal generalisation. Apéry's own printed account is a 3-page sketch (1979) plus a continued-fraction-interpolation paper (1981); the complete proofs in circulation are due to van der Poorten, Cohen and Reyssat (with Zagier's and Lenstra's input).

### Cited Findings

**Structure of the argument (as fixed by the literature)**
- All known proofs have the same skeleton: construct rationals u_n, v_n with (1) limsup |u_nζ(3) − v_n|^{1/n} ≤ (√2−1)⁴ = 0.0294372…, (2) u_n ∈ Z and 2d_n³v_n ∈ Z, (3) the form is non-zero infinitely often; the conclusion follows because (√2−1)⁴e³ < 1 (prime number theorem in the form log d_n ~ n) — [Fischler, Sém. Bourbaki 910, §1](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066)
- The prime number theorem can be replaced by the elementary bound d_n < 3.23ⁿ for large n, since (√2−1)⁴·3.23³ < 1 — [Fischler, Remark 1.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066); Zudilin uses the still cruder d_n < 3ⁿ with 3³(√2−1)⁴ = 0.7948… < 1 — [Zudilin, math/0202159](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0202159); the Coq formalisation proves the needed lcm bound by formalising an elementary argument of Hanson (1972) instead of the PNT — [Mahboubi–Sibut-Pinote](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.06611)
- The recurrence: (n+1)³y_{n+1} − (34n³+51n²+27n+5)y_n + n³y_{n−1} = 0 with u_0=1, u_1=5, v_0=0, v_1=6; characteristic polynomial X²−34X+1 with roots (√2±1)⁴, so u_n^{1/n}, v_n^{1/n} → (√2+1)⁴ = 33.9705627… and u_nζ(3)−v_n is the (unique up to scalar) minimal solution, of size (√2−1)^{4n} — [Fischler §1.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066)
- First values: u_n = 1, 5, 73, 1445, 33001, 819005, …; v_n = 0, 6, 351/4, 62531/36, 11424695/288, … — [Fischler §1](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066)
- Casoratian identity: the determinant Δ_n = v_nu_{n−1} − u_nv_{n−1} equals 6/n³ (van der Poorten's viewpoint), i.e. v_n/u_n − v_{n−1}/u_{n−1} = 6/(n³u_nu_{n−1}); hence ζ(3) = Σ_{k≥1} 6/(k³u_ku_{k−1}) with v_n/u_n as n-th partial sum — [Fischler §1.1 and Remark 1.3](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066)
- Continued-fraction form: ζ(3) = 6/(5 − 1/(117 − 64/(535 − … − n⁶/(34n³+51n²+27n+5) − …))), obtainable by a general convergence-acceleration procedure for continued fractions (Apéry 1981; Batut–Olivier; Zeilberger) — [Fischler §1.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066)
- Explicit formulas: u_n = Σ_k C(n,k)²C(n+k,k)²; v_n = Σ_k C(n,k)²C(n+k,k)²·(Σ_{m≤n} 1/m³ + Σ_{m≤k} (−1)^{m−1}/(2m³C(n,m)C(n+m,m))). Integrality 2d_n³v_n ∈ Z follows from a p-adic valuation count on binomials; the recurrence is verified by exhibiting telescoping double sequences A_{n,k}, B_{n,k}, e.g. A_{n,k} = 4(2n+1)(k(2k+1) − (2n+1)²)C(n,k)²C(n+k,k)² (finding them "n'a pas été une tâche facile", see van der Poorten §7; this is the problem that motivated Zeilberger's algorithms) — [Fischler §1.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066)
- The famous series ζ(3) = (5/2)Σ(−1)^{n−1}/(n³C(2n,n)) is *not* used in the irrationality proof; analogous accelerated series exist for ζ(5) (e.g. ζ(5) = (5/2)Σ_{n≥1} (−1)^n/(n³C(2n,n))·(Σ_{j<n}1/j² − 4/(5n²))), "mais aucune de ces généralisations n'a permis d'obtenir de nouveau résultat d'irrationalité : la croissance des dénominateurs est trop rapide par rapport à la convergence" — [Fischler §1.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066)
- Quantitatively Apéry's forms give irrationality exponent μ(ζ(3)) ≤ 13.4179 (ζ(3) is not Liouville) — [Fischler §3.1](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066); the value is quoted as μ(ζ(3)) < 13.41782… (and μ(ζ(2)) < 11.850878…) — [Dauguet–Zudilin, arXiv:1401.5322](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.5322)
- Calegari–Dimitrov–Tang's reformulation: Apéry writes down power series A(x), B(x) with a_n ∈ Z, [1,…,n]³b_n ∈ Z; A, B have radius of convergence (√2−1)⁴ while P = B − ζ(3)A has radius exactly (√2+1)⁴; irrationality follows from the single inequality 4 log(√2+1) > 3, with exponent δ = (4log(√2+1)−3)/(4log(√2+1)+3); "Apéry's proof uses very little number theory" (weak PNT + an elementary lemma) — [Calegari–Dimitrov–Tang, arXiv:2408.15403, §1.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403)

### Inferences
- The numerical margin is thin but real: log-size of the linear form is −4log(1+√2) ≈ −3.5255 per step versus +3 from d_n³, a net −0.5255; any construction for ζ(5) of the same shape needs d_n⁵ (cost +5) and hence a much faster-decaying form. (Computed from the constants cited above; the exponent 13.4178 = 1 + (3.5255+3)/(3.5255−3) agrees with the cited value.)
- The first-generation write-ups (van der Poorten, Cohen, Reyssat) are one proof, not three: recurrence ⇄ explicit binomial sums, glued by telescoping certificates.

## 2. Beukers' integral proof and its descendants: irrationality measures (Dvornicich–Viola, Hata, Rhin–Viola, 2026 preprints), modular-form and K3 proofs, Padé approximation

### Takeaway
Beukers' triple integral reproduces exactly Apéry's linear forms and is the root of the whole quantitative line (Hata → Rhin–Viola group method, μ(ζ(3)) < 5.513891, the published record since 2001); two unrefereed 2026 preprints claim improvements (5.5138800 and 5.4197). Beukers' modular-form proof is the one construction that is "individually" tied to ζ(3) as an L-value rather than as a polylogarithm value, which is the documented reason it does not transfer to ζ(5).

### Cited Findings

**Beukers' integrals**
- **F. Beukers, "A note on the irrationality of ζ(2) and ζ(3)", Bull. London Math. Soc. 11 (1979), no. 3, 268–272.** [V-B] DOI 10.1112/blms/11.3.268 (DOI from search index; Wiley page not opened); bibliographic data in [Fischler [Be1]](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066), [Zudilin](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0202159). Result/method: the integral I_n = ∫∫∫_{[0,1]³} uⁿ(1−u)ⁿvⁿ(1−v)ⁿwⁿ(1−w)ⁿ/(1−w(1−uv))^{n+1} du dv dw; the maximum of u(1−u)v(1−v)w(1−w)/(1−w(1−uv)) on the cube is (√2−1)⁴, giving the size; n-fold integrations by parts turn it into −∫∫ log(uv)/(1−uv)·P_n(u)P_n(v) du dv with Legendre polynomials P_n, and ∫∫ −log(uv)/(1−uv)·u^k v^l du dv ∈ 2Zζ(3) + d_n^{−3}Z, whence I_n = 2(u_nζ(3) − v_n) with the *same* u_n, v_n as Apéry — [Fischler §1.3](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066). **Padé-approximation formulations (Beukers and after)**
- **F. Beukers, "Padé-approximations in number theory", in Padé Approximation and its Applications (Amsterdam 1980), Lecture Notes in Math. 888, Springer 1981, 90–99.** [V-B] — [Fischler [Be2]](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066), [Lai [4]](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06838). Padé problem: find polynomials A_n, B_n, C_n, D_n of degree ≤ n with A_nLi₂(1/z)+B_nLi₁(1/z)+D_n = O(z^{−n−1}), 2A_nLi₃(1/z)+B_nLi₂(1/z)+C_n = O(z^{−n−1}) and B_n(1) = 0; the solution is unique up to scalar and gives Apéry's forms at z = 1; the underlying operator is the order-4 hypergeometric operator z(δ+n+1)²(δ−n)² − δ⁴ — [Fischler §1.6](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066). Relation: reformulation (explains *why* the rational function R_n(X) = ((X−1)…(X−n))²/(X(X+1)…(X+n))² is forced).
- **F. Beukers, "The values of polylogarithms", Colloq. Math. Soc. János Bolyai 34 (1984), 219–228** [V-B] — [Fischler [Be3]](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066).

[V-B]

### Inferences
- Among all post-1979 constructions, only the Rhin–Viola family (and its hypergeometric/cellular/CMF re-encodings) changes the *linear forms*; Beukers 1979, the Padé formulations, Prévost and the modular proof all return Apéry's u_n, v_n.
- If either 2026 preprint is confirmed, the ζ(3) record would no longer be Rhin–Viola's; the two claims are of very different nature (a 10⁻⁵ re-optimisation inside the Rhin–Viola family versus a new ₅F₄-type trajectory giving ≈0.094 improvement). Neither has, as far as the retrieved pages show, been refereed.
- The modular proof's dependence on L(F,3) = −2ζ(3)ζ(0) for an Eisenstein-type weight-4 form explains structurally why it has produced ζ(3), ζ(2), L-values and p-adic analogues but no ζ(5): there is no documented weight/level where ζ(5) arises with a compatible Hauptmodul and d_n-type denominators (this is my reading of Fischler's Remark 1.5 and of Beukers' "disappointingly low" yield, not a quoted theorem).

## 3. Hypergeometric / series proofs: Gutnik, Nesterenko, Ball's series, Zudilin, Sorokin, Zeilberger (WZ), Krattenthaler–Rivoal, later Nesterenko

### Takeaway
The "hypergeometric" proofs (Gutnik/Beukers/Nesterenko series Σ R_n'(k), Nesterenko's Meijer-G contour integral, Ball's very-well-poised series, Sorokin's multiple-polylogarithm Padé problem) all specialise at z = 1 to Apéry's linear forms; their value for ζ(5) is that they are the proofs that *do* generalise to linear forms in several odd zeta values (Ball–Rivoal, Zudilin), while documenting precisely where single-value irrationality is lost (extra factor of d_n, and inability to kill all but one zeta value).

### Inferences
- Every proof in this family is a reformulation of Apéry's at z = 1 (Fischler's "on construit toujours les mêmes formes linéaires"), but they are *functionally* different away from z = 1 (different polylogarithm combinations), and that is what makes Nesterenko's and Ball's versions generalisable to several zeta values.
- The documented failure mode for ζ(5) in this family is twofold: (a) a single well-poised series of the right size carries ζ(3) along with ζ(5) (order-3 recursion, two "parasitic" directions); (b) denominators are D_n⁵ or worse against a decay rate far too slow (0.3375ⁿ·e^{5n} → ∞ in Zudilin's recursion).

### Gaps
- Nesterenko (1996, 2009), Sorokin (1994, 1998, 2002), Gutnik (1979, 1983) and Prévost (1996) full texts were not opened; descriptions are from Fischler's survey and search-index abstracts. In particular I could not verify from the source what exactly Nesterenko's 2009 "twice as dense" sequence is.
- Whether Krattenthaler–Rivoal's memoir contains an explicit self-contained derivation of the d_n³ bound for Ball's ζ(3) series (thus answering Rivoal's question quoted by Zudilin) was not checked in the text; only the abstract was read.

## 4. Other viewpoints: continued fractions (Apéry, Ramanujan, Nesterenko), disconjugacy, formal proofs (Coq, Isabelle, Lean), Rajkumar, Brown's moduli-space integrals, Calegari–Dimitrov–Tang, Apéry-like recurrences (Zagier, Almkvist–Zudilin), Calabi–Yau/motivic interpretations, and 2020–2026 developments

### Takeaway
These works mostly re-derive or re-interpret the same Apéry sequences (continued-fraction acceleration from Ramanujan's fraction; WZ/telescoping; periods of M_{0,6}; Picard–Fuchs/K3; higher normal functions) or machine-check an existing proof (Coq: Apéry's route; Isabelle and Lean 4: Beukers' route). The genuinely new ideas after 2014 are structural — Brown's cellular integrals, Calegari–Dimitrov–Tang's arithmetic holonomy bounds (which reuse Apéry-type data when the Apéry inequality fails), and, per autumn-2026 preprints, Hankel-determinant methods credited to A. Fauzan's claimed proof of ζ(5) ∉ Q.

**Moduli spaces M_{0,n}, cellular integrals, motives**
- **F. Brown, "Irrationality proofs for zeta values, moduli spaces and dinner parties", Mosc. J. Comb. **Arithmetic holonomy bounds (Calegari–Dimitrov–Tang)**
- **F. Calegari, V. Dimitrov, Y. ## 6. Cross-cutting question: which proofs are genuinely different, and what is documented about why they do or do not extend to ζ(5)?

### Takeaway
The literature itself says the proofs of Apéry's theorem all construct the same approximations; the documented reasons none of them reached ζ(5) are (i) parasitic zeta/MZV terms that cannot all be removed, (ii) denominators d_n⁵ (or one power of d_n too many) against insufficient decay, (iii) the modular proof's dependence on ζ(3) being a specific L-value, and (iv) rarity of Apéry-type integrality. As of November 2026 several preprints report that ζ(5) ∉ Q has been obtained by a determinant method rather than by any Apéry-type sequence — unverified by me.

### Cited Findings
- "Toutes les preuves connues de l'irrationalité de ζ(3) ont la même structure … on construit toujours les mêmes formes linéaires" — [Fischler §1](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066); "Many proofs … all of which construct the same sequences, a_n and b_n, by vastly different methods … none of these proofs have generalisations to higher zeta values" — [Rajkumar](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.5881); "According to Fischler's survey, all these proofs share a common structure" — [Mahboubi–Sibut-Pinote §3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.06611).
- (i) Parasitic terms: generic M_{0,6} periods involve 1, ζ(2), ζ(3) and the "entire difficulty" is making the ζ(2) coefficient vanish; in weight 5 the generic integral has 20 parameters and involves all MZVs of weight ≤ 5 — [Brown §1.3–1.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1412.6508); in the M_{0,8} family ζ(2) is "parasitic" and the leading period is ζ(5)+2ζ(3)ζ(2) — [Brown–Zudilin](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.03391); well-poised symmetry removes only even zeta values and "on ne sait pas du tout la généraliser" — [Fischler §2.4](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066); Zudilin's ζ(5) series carries ζ(3) and yields an order-3 recursion — [Zudilin math/0206178](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0206178).
- (ii) Denominators versus decay: accelerated series for ζ(5) fail because "la croissance des dénominateurs est trop rapide par rapport à la convergence" — [Fischler §1.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066); Ball's series for ζ(3) a priori needs d_n⁴ and (√2−1)⁴e⁴ > 1 — [Fischler §1.9](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066); for ζ(5) the inclusions q_n ∈ Z, 2D_n⁵p_n ∈ Z "do not allow oneself to prove the irrationality" — [Zudilin math/0206178](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0206178); best effective approximations before 2026 had exponent 0.86 < 1 — [Brown–Zudilin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03391); for general L-values "the analogous inequality … always seems to fail" — [Calegari–Dimitrov–Tang §1.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2408.15403).
- (iii) The modular proof is tied "individuellement" to ζ(3) = L(F,3) — [Fischler Remark 1.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0303066).
- (iv) Rarity/structure: WZ-generated Apéry-style schemes "usually [are] of higher order, and usually [do] not yield irrationality" — [Zeilberger §13](https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~zeilberg/mamarim/mamarimTeX/pun.tex); Apéry's acceleration applied elsewhere gives new continued fractions "without any Diophantine consequences" — [Cohen 2024](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.17720); no homogeneous polynomials of degree > 3 satisfy the conditions of the 2×2 recurrence construction — [Rajkumar](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1212.5881); no three-term Apéry-like recursion is expected for (Archimedean) ζ(5), four-term ones are known — [Lai–Sprang–Zudilin](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.05005); automated searches found many new irrational constants but not ζ(5) — [Ekhad–Zeilberger](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.4445), [Dougherty-Bliss–Koutschan–Zeilberger](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.08308).
- What did extend from the ζ(3) proofs: p-adic ζ(3) (Calegari; Beukers) — [arXiv:math/0408214](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0408214), [arXiv:math/0603277](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0603277); infinitely many odd zeta values / one of ζ(5),…,ζ(11) (Nesterenko–Ball template) — [Zudilin math/0206176](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0206176); 2-adic ζ(5) by an Apéry-type three-term recursion — [Lai–Sprang–Zudilin](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05005); L(2,χ₋₃) via holonomy bounds on Zagier's sequences — [Calegari–Dimitrov–Tang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.15403).
- November-2026 status as stated in retrieved arXiv abstracts/introductions: "the proof that ζ(5) is irrational posted by Fauzan on September 17, 2026" using a "Hankel determinant method" — [Fischler–Lai–Sprang–Zudilin, arXiv:2611.16156](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16156); "Fauzan's determinantal proof that ζ(5) is irrational also yields |ζ(5)−a/b| > b^{−260}" — [Giraudin, arXiv:2611.01708](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01708); in contrast, [Leibtag et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27799) argue that classical Apéry-type sequences "still" yield new theorems (for ζ(3), ζ(4) combinations), while describing the determinant methods as having "abandoned the classical constructions".

### Inferences
- Apéry's theorem has, in effect, one construction (the Apéry forms) with roughly ten derivations, plus one genuinely larger family (Rhin–Viola and its re-encodings). Bibliographies that list "many proofs" should be read with that in mind.
- The decisive documented difference between ζ(3) and ζ(5), on all routes, is not analytic smallness (available) but arithmetic cost: one needs either a vanishing theorem that isolates ζ(5) or p-adic savings beyond the entrywise d_n-bounds. The 2026 determinant method, as described by Lai, addresses precisely the second point ("the determinant structure strengthens the p-adic estimates").
